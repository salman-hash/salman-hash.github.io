A brain-computer interface project using the MindRove Arc EEG headset.

## What I did

- **Captured and decoded EEG data** to detect hand and arm movements, such as gripping and walking, using MATLAB, Python and the MindRove app.
- Analysed how **brain signals change during motor actions**, for real-time interpretation of the data.
