SceneScribe helps visually impaired people understand and move through unfamiliar spaces. A head-worn camera and a sensor-equipped smart cane feed a set of vision-language-model agents. The agents describe the scene, answer questions about it and guide navigation, without overwhelming the user with information.

## What I built

- An **AI-powered wearable perception and navigation system** using vision-language models (VLMs) and real-time environmental sensing.
- A **multi-agent VLM architecture** that splits scene understanding, navigation and contextual assistance across specialised agents.
- A **custom smart cane** that combines camera-based perception with ESP32 control, ultrasonic sensing, servo actuation and haptic feedback.
- The **wearable and smart-cane hardware**, designed in CAD and prototyped with 3D printing.

## Outcomes

- Paper: *Multi-Agent Situational Awareness for Visually Impaired*, IEEE IBCAST 2024 ([DOI 10.1109/IBCAST61650.2024.10877205](https://doi.org/10.1109/IBCAST61650.2024.10877205)).
- Utility patent filed: *Adaptive AI-enabled Framework for Navigation of Visually Impaired Persons*.
- Awarded the **Rector's Gold Medal** for the best final year project at NUST.
