As Autonomous Vehicles Team Lead at NUST Automotive Group, I ran a 50-member team building the driverless stack for a Formula Student car.

## Responsibilities

- Led the team across **perception, planning, control and system integration**.
- Led **system integration** work on the CAN bus, LiDAR and stereo sensing, and path identification, connecting the perception and control subsystems.
- Ran **technical training** and mentored 20+ members on autonomous-vehicle architecture, subsystem integration and development workflows.
