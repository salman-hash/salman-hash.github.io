A competition robot that follows a line-marked course, removes rocks from given positions and plants trees in their place.

## What I did

- Designed the **line-following navigation** and the **path-planning logic**, combining predefined paths to choose a route through the target points.
- Built a **steel structure with a bar mechanism** for picking and placing.

## Result

- **Runner-up** at the Mini National Engineering Robotics Contest 2024.
